Adapted Assets:
- droplets_mask.tif: droplet normal map (RG), random phase offset (B), height/mask (A)
- rivulets_mask.tif: rivulet normal map (RG), flow distortion vectors (BA)
- CanopyRain.shader: adapted shader algorithm featuring droplet strike & erosion animation,
  dual-rest-field flow distortion for continuous seamless rivulets, adapted for Unity URP,
  local airflow orientation, and dynamic aircraft speed.
