Recorded pilot voices: sources and licences
===========================================

Every clip in this folder is cut and filtered from a Creative Commons 0 (public domain) recording by
tools/Build-SoundClips.py. Licences were checked on the source pages on 2026-10-10. CC0 needs no credit;
the authors are named here as thanks and so the clips can be rebuilt.

keh0-keh4.wav   "strenuous_grunts" by takareads
                https://freesound.org/people/takareads/sounds/841313/            CC0
gasp0.wav       "AS060822_Short Exertion" by SuddenDice
                https://freesound.org/people/SuddenDice/sounds/558618/           CC0
gasp1.wav       "Masked_Breathing_Raspy_Loop" by Mediasaur
recovery.wav    https://freesound.org/people/Mediasaur/sounds/788055/            CC0
heart.wav       "Heartbeat sounds" (heartbeat_fast_0.wav) by bart
                https://opengameart.org/content/heartbeat-sounds                 CC0

Processing: mono, 22050 Hz, 16-bit. Voices get a mask filter (high-pass 180 Hz, +4 dB near 1.2 kHz,
low-pass 6.5 kHz, a 4 ms resonance); the heartbeat is band-limited to 25-400 Hz. All are levelled to -3 dBFS.
The source downloads are not kept in the repository.
