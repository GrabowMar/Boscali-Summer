# Radio module

Owns local library discovery, starter stations, playback, the vanilla-music handoff, the MFD
UI, icons, assets, and its Harmony patches.

Radio is client-local and must not send multiplayer data. Never download, bundle, extract,
log, or transmit music. Keep the path-containment, catalogue, decode, clip-retention and
icon-size bounds intact. Update the radio entries in `docs/DESIGN_NOTES.md` and
`docs/ROADMAP.md` only when a verified radio compatibility seam or public radio behaviour
changes.
