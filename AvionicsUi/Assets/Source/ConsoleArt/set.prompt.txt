Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one finished abstract decorative background plate for a military flight simulator's multifunction display. This is artwork only, never a complete UI or mockup.
Style and art direction: richly layered engraved tactical atlas mixed with elegant scientific field visualization. Deliberate sophisticated asymmetry, a coherent flowing composition with large and medium-scale structures, varied fine line weights, fine secondary hatching, overlapping offset construction geometries and delicate meshes. It must remain visually interesting when reduced to 512 pixels wide. Rich detail and spatial depth, but restrained enough to sit behind a user interface. Several scales of detail, rhythm and hierarchy; do not produce a simplistic isolated icon or a few repeated curves.
Palette: strictly monochrome white and silver-gray linework on perfectly pure black. No color whatsoever. Most lines middle-gray, selected structural lines brighter, broad layered shapes only dim gray, no bright solid masses.
Composition: landscape 3:2 (1536x1024). Upper fifth of image completely empty pure black. Artwork flows across lower two thirds with soft airy negative spaces and broad gradual fading into pure black at all four edges. No frame, no rectangle around drawing, no border. The composition should feel like part of a larger subtle technical field, never a central logo.
Text: absolutely no text, labels, letters, numbers, symbols resembling text, readable data or watermark.
Avoid: physical objects, equipment, buildings, people, vehicles, weaponry, photo scenes, literal charts or paper, centered emblem, giant pictogram, simple icon, random noise, fake buttons or screen panels, generic sci-fi concentric circles.
Subject: layered abstract adjustment structures made from nested offset faceted channels, oblique cross-sections and fine woven calibration-like grids. Different scales of precise broken geometry interlock with curved filaments and subtle hatching to suggest refinement and configuration without representing equipment. Build an elegant spatial composition with overlapping levels and open gaps. No literal exploded hardware, circuit board, gears, monitor, equipment frame or central settings icon.
