# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — first public alpha

- **Wing Command is built in.** Remove the stand-alone Wing Command mod; AI wingmen, the WMC
  page and formations now ship inside Boscali Summer.
- **VANGUARD weapons** (needs Blueprinter): AEGIS-3 pod, railgun, GLAIVE-2 UGV carrier, ORCA
  water runner, ALE-X towed decoy, and the SKYWELL service glider for air-to-air refuel/rearm
  from Tarantula and MC-260 Chimera cargo bays.
- **CG-9 HAMILCAR cargo glider** (VANGUARD, needs Blueprinter): release it from a Tarantula or
  MC-260 Chimera cargo bay at a designated ground target. Helped along by a small pusher
  propeller, it flies fifteen metres for every metre of height (up to 45 km), lands on its belly
  about 250 m short of the target and a Hexhound GMG rolls off as a live unit. It needs flat dry
  ground; anything that hits it on the way in loses both.
- **Airborne** (needs Blueprinter): HALO paratroopers, Ibis fast-rope and rappel insertions.
- **OPS fronts**: SPACE / CYBER / SOF fronts with directives, programmes and readiness.
- Reworked HUD, interaction menu, OPS/STR/COMMS consoles, maps, clouds and effects.
- Player build: developer cheats and tools are locked off and hidden from the F1 window.
