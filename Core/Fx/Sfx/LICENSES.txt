Recorded effect clips: sources and licences
===========================================

Every clip in this folder is cut from a Creative Commons 0 (public domain) recording by tools/Build-SoundClips.py.
Licences were checked on the source pages on 2026-10-10. CC0 needs no credit; the authors are named here as
thanks and so the clips can be rebuilt. The source downloads are not kept in the repository.

Processing: mono, 22050 Hz, 16-bit, rumble below 40 Hz removed, levelled to -3 dBFS.

Decoded but not shipped: "Tire.ogg" by egomassive (https://freesound.org/people/egomassive/sounds/536769/, CC0).
The game's own skid loop already squeals at touchdown, so a second chirp would double it.

The kaiju (kaiju-*.wav) is made by `synth` in tools/Build-SoundClips.py from CC0 recordings and synthesis, nothing else:
voices layer a synthesized growling throat with "strenuous_grunts" by takareads (841313), "AS060822_Short Exertion" by
SuddenDice (558618) and "Masked_Breathing_Raspy_Loop" by Mediasaur (788055), slowed three to four times (sources in
modules/Immersion/Assets/Voice/LICENSES.txt); the shriek adds the leather creak above, the footfall the building collapse
above, the laser charge a slowed breath. The beam, spike and claw sounds are pure synthesis.

                       the crack, rolling thunder tail and debris patter layered around it are synthesized (scratch mix, numpy).
